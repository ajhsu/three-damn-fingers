Three Damn Fingers

macOS-style three-finger drag for Windows Precision Touchpads

Coming soon to the Microsoft Store

What it does

  • Drag with three fingers. Put three fingers on the touchpad and move them. The app holds the mouse button down, and the pointer follows your fingers. Lift your fingers to drop. Drag windows, select text, and move files with no press and hold.
  • Tap with three fingers to click the middle mouse button, for example to open a link in a new tab.
  • Tune it live. The settings window shows your fingers on the touchpad while you touch it. Set the speed, the acceleration, the finger travel before a drag starts, and a release delay. The delay lets you lift your fingers and put them back to continue the same drag.

Before you start

You need Windows 10 or Windows 11 and a Windows Precision Touchpad. Set the Windows three-finger gestures to "Nothing": open Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Touchpad > Three-finger gestures, and set Swipes and Taps to Nothing. The app warns you when they are on.

Windows blocks input to apps that run as administrator, such as Task Manager. The drag does not work over their windows.
