Three Damn Fingers

Windows still has no goddamn three-finger drag. This goddamn app adds it.

What it does

  • Drag with three goddamn fingers. Put three fingers on the touchpad, move them, and lift. The app holds the mouse button for you while your fingers move. Move windows, files, and text, and never hold a goddamn button again.
  • Tap with three fingers to middle-click, for example to open a link in a new tab.
  • It tells you if the goddamn thing works. The settings window shows your fingers on the touchpad when you test it. Set the speed, the acceleration, and how far your fingers move before a drag starts. A lift delay lets you lift your fingers and put them back to keep dragging. Changes apply at once.

Before you start

You need Windows 10 or Windows 11 and a Windows Precision Touchpad. Open Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Touchpad > Three-finger gestures, and set Swipes and Taps to Nothing. If you forget, the app tells you. Loudly.

Windows blocks input to apps that run as administrator, such as Task Manager. The drag does not work over their windows.
