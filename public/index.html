Before you start

  1. Get the app.

    It is free in the Microsoft Store. It needs a Windows Precision Touchpad.

  2. Turn off the Windows gestures.

    Open Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Touchpad > Three-finger gestures. Set Swipes and Taps to Nothing. If you forget, the app tells you. Loudly.

  3. Drag.

    Put three fingers on the touchpad and move them. Lift them to drop.

Windows blocks input to apps that run as administrator, such as Task Manager. The drag does not work over their windows.
