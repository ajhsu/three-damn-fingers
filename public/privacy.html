Three Damn Fingers

Privacy policy

Last changed: November 27, 2026

Three Damn Fingers does not collect, send, or share any personal information. The app has no network features. All its data stays on your PC.

What the app reads

  • Touchpad input: the positions and the timing of your fingers on a Windows Precision Touchpad. The app uses this input only to find three-finger drags and taps. It keeps the input in memory and does not save it, except in the detailed log and in recordings. See the next section.
  • Pointer position: the app reads the mouse pointer position and moves the pointer during a drag.
  • Windows settings: the touchpad gesture settings and the color theme of the taskbar. The app warns you about gestures that conflict with it, and it matches its tray icon to the taskbar.

What the app saves on your PC

  • Settings: the choices that you make in the settings window.
  • Log: app events, such as start and stop, the touchpads that the app uses, drags, warnings, and errors. At the "debug" and "trace" log levels, the log also holds pointer positions. At "trace", it also holds every touchpad frame. The log starts over at 10 MB and keeps one old copy.
  • Recordings: only when you start the app with the --record option. A recording holds finger positions, timing, and pointer positions.
  • Start with Windows: when you turn it on, the app asks Windows to start it when you sign in.

The Microsoft Store version saves its files in its own data folder. Windows removes this folder when you uninstall the app. Other versions save their files in %APPDATA%\ThreeDamnFingers and %LOCALAPPDATA%\ThreeDamnFingers. The files are in your user profile, and the permissions of your Windows account protect them.

What the app sends

Nothing. The app does not connect to the internet. It has no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. It shares no data with anyone, including the developer.

Microsoft Store

When you get the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download and the updates. The Microsoft Privacy Statement covers the data that Windows and the Store collect. Microsoft can give the developer reports, such as the number of installs and data about app crashes.

This website

This website has no cookies and no ads. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics: a small script from Cloudflare counts the visits and measures how fast the pages load. It sets no cookies, and Cloudflare says that it collects no personal data. Cloudflare hosts the site, and handles each request to deliver the pages and to protect the site from attacks. For up to seven days, Cloudflare keeps a log of the requests, with the page, the time, the browser, and the IP address, so that the developer can find faults. The Cloudflare Privacy Policy covers that data.

Your control

  • Open the settings file and the log folder from the settings window. You can read and delete the files at any time.
  • Set the log detail with log_level in the settings file.
  • Uninstall the app in Settings > Apps > Installed apps. Windows removes the Store version and its data folder.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

When the app or this website changes how it uses data, this page changes too. The date at the top shows the last change.
